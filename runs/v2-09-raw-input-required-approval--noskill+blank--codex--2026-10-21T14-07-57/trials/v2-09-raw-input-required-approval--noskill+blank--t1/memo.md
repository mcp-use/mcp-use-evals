The decisive miss was the cancellation/decline wording: the non-accept branch returned `terminalError("Deployment was not approved.")` (`src/server.ts`), while the grader required the final result to contain “decline”; the transcript’s live check showed `"text":"Deployment was not approved."`. The agent treated that response as sufficient, reporting `verified: initial form, accepted approval with note, terminal decline`, so its verification checked terminality but not the expected decline wording. By contrast, `approve: false` used `terminalError("Deployment was declined.")` (`src/server.ts`), creating inconsistent messages for two decline paths.
